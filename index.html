✈ SKY SQUADRON

Keyboard + mouse — click to capture the mouse, then point where you want to fly  |  left button: guns  |  right button: missiles
Shift: afterburner  |  C: air brake  |  R: skill  |  Q / E: rudder  |  W A S D: fly by hand instead  |  Space / F: guns / missiles  |  Esc: pause

Gamepad — left stick: pitch & roll (pull back to climb)  |  right stick or LB / RB: rudder  |  RT: guns  |  LT: missiles
A: afterburner  |  B: air brake  |  Y: skill  |  View/Back: invert pitch  |  Start: pause

Hangar — mouse: left click places a part on the face you point at, right click removes, middle click paints, right-drag turns the view, wheel zooms
gamepad: stick moves the cursor, LB / RB layer, LT / RT part, A place, B remove, X paint, Y mirror, Start ready  |  keys: WASD, Q/E, Z/C, Space, X, F, V, R
